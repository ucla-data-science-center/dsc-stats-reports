Research Capability Built

Pathway: how DSC instruction builds research capability at UCLA and across the University of California

Finding and scope

DSC instruction grew from workshops for its own campus into instruction co-run across the University of California, and it trained campus volunteers who went on to lead much of the teaching. The evidence covers workshops from May 2017 through September 2026 and counts registrations and sessions, not individual learning outcomes.

Evidence in brief

27,986

Registered seats, May 2017 to September 2026 (2026 partial)

Registrations, not unique people or attendance.

Two supporting measures:

  • 819 workshop sessions, May 2017 to May 2026 (a session is one distinct event and date).
  • At least 39 people from two separately defined UC Carpentries cohorts (September 2025, and 2021, 2022 and 2024) were subsequently observed in research or data-intensive roles, from public evidence. This is an early trajectory signal, not a tracer study; it does not show that the workshops caused those roles.

A program example: the UC Carpentries network

What it is. In 2020 DSC co-founded the systemwide UC Carpentries network with partner campuses at Berkeley, San Diego, Santa Barbara, Merced, and Riverside, turning a single-campus workshop program into instruction delivered across the University of California.

What DSC does. Co-coordinates the series, recruits and trains instructors, and co-delivers workshops. Campus volunteers trained through the program now lead much of the teaching.

Scale. The UC Carpentries series drew 10,749 registered seats from 2020 through September 2026 (see the seat table on the instruction evidence page).

Limits. Registrations are not people or attendance, the audience spans all participating campuses, and credit for the network is shared with its partners. Individual learning outcomes are not measured here.

ImportantWhat this pattern means, and what it cannot mean
  • It means DSC instruction reached a large and changing audience: its own campus through 2019, then UC-wide online series from 2020, with campus-trained volunteers teaching alongside staff.
  • It cannot mean that 27,986 people were trained. A registered seat is one registration for one session. One person who registers for four sessions counts four times, and many registrants do not attend.
  • Attendance is recorded only for some series. Where it exists, compare attended with registered seats within that series and those dates only. The UC Carpentries ratio (about 1 attended per 3 registered) does not transfer to other series or to the total.
  • UCLA participants are a subset. The UC-wide series include other campuses’ registrants; UCLA-only figures are on the instruction evidence page.
  • The lower 2025-2026 bars are not a measure of demand. 2026 is partial, and neither GIS Week nor Love Data Week has counted DSC seats in those years (DSC was not involved in GIS Week in 2024-2025, while its GIS position was unfilled).
NoteThe long view (history, as of 2026)

Instruction went through three eras. From 2017 the unit taught locally, one campus, in person, and by January 2020 campus volunteers trained through the program were leading the workshops. In 2020 the pandemic made cross-campus coordination cheap, and the systemwide UC Carpentries network was co-founded in response, with Berkeley, San Diego, Santa Barbara, Merced, and Riverside as partners. From 2023 that network operated at system scale, a program other campuses schedule around. Read the teaching section of the history
