WarningKnown limitations — Dataverse infrastructure (migration in progress)

These impact metrics undercount real scholarly reach due to known infrastructure issues being addressed in an ongoing Dataverse migration:

  • Dataverse 5.14 DataCite export bug: The publication metadata field (paper DOI linked by depositors) is not exported to DataCite as relatedIdentifiers. As a result, UCLA Dataverse datasets show 0 citations in DataCite, even when they are cited. Citation counts here are retrieved directly from OpenAlex as a workaround.
  • Make Data Count (MDC) not enabled: Per-dataset view and download metrics are unavailable. Only aggregate download totals are reported.
  • Restricted and draft datasets excluded: ~562 of 942 datasets in the index are inaccessible without authentication and are not included in this analysis.
  • ISBN-linked datasets not tracked: 160 datasets are linked to book chapters (via ISBN). These represent real scholarship connections — primarily from large archaeology collections — but citation counts are not retrievable via OpenAlex.
  • Depositor coverage gap: ~200 publicly accessible datasets have no publication link at all. Improving intake guidance for depositors would increase coverage.

Expected improvements after migration to Dataverse 6.x: DataCite relatedIdentifiers export fixed, MDC enablement possible.

NoteData notes
  • AWS costs: Cost Explorer API (last 12 months) merged with historical CSV exports. Most recent 1–2 months are estimates; final billing may differ
  • Dataverse datasets/files/downloads: Cumulative counts from the UCLA Dataverse /api/info/metrics endpoint — stored in data/raw/infrastructure/datasets_files_published_monthly.csv
  • Downloads: File-level events as reported by Dataverse. Includes automated and bot traffic; not filtered for human-only access
  • S3 storage: Fetched live from AWS CloudWatch at render time. May be unavailable without valid AWS credentials; CloudWatch reports storage metrics once daily
  • Scholarship impact: Publication DOIs from Dataverse metadata API; citation counts from OpenAlex. Undercounts real impact — see limitations callout above.
