Research Enabled by DSC

What the UCLA Library Data Science Center contributes to research at UCLA, and the evidence behind each figure

UCLA researchers have used DSC for data and technical work that crosses lab and department boundaries. At least 1,908 confirmed UCLA researchers used its instruction or consultation from 2017 to September 2026. DSC also made a 10 TB national voter file analysis-ready, provides governed access to restricted data, and operates UCLA’s research-data repository. Its staff time, counting DSC staff and time contributed by other Library units, fell from 5.7 FTE in 2022-23 to 4.0 in 2025-26. The question for the university is who will be accountable for these functions as DSC’s capacity changes: connecting them, setting standards, maintaining the infrastructure, and carrying the expertise forward.

What the evidence establishes

DSC’s work shows up in research outputs, not only in service counts. Three documented collaborations on the Research enabled page show different kinds of DSC support: data preparation and computing for a helicopter-surveillance study, where one data-loading step ran about 159 times faster; a national deaths-in-custody dataset a lab now updates itself; and 3D capture of a refugee boat for a humanities initiative. More are on the shared investments page. How cases are selected.

Three ways DSC contributes

Research enabled

Researchers across campus bring data problems to DSC for consultation, and some engagements become multi-year collaborations.

about 1,850 recorded consultations, 2017 to September 2026. Records are incomplete after mid-2024, so later years cannot measure demand.

Research capability built

DSC moved from teaching its own campus to co-running instruction across the University of California, and campus volunteers it trained went on to lead much of the teaching.

819 workshop sessions, May 2017 to May 2026. Registered seats by program are charted on the pathway page.

Shared investments

DSC runs UCLA Dataverse and governed access to restricted data, the current form of the stewardship that began in 1961.

about 1,390 released datasets in UCLA Dataverse, Feb 2026: about 376 deposited directly at UCLA and about 1,014 legacy SSDA datasets that Library staff harvested and curate.

Research supported by shared investments

Since 1961. UCLA has kept research data for reuse since 1961, when political scientist Dwaine Marvick founded the Political Behavior Archive. The unit’s name and institutional home have changed several times since; what persisted is the stewardship of research data and the work of widening access to it. Read the history

The materials have changed, from survey datasets to large and restricted files, spatial imagery, 3D models, and research code, and the archive’s knowledge now shapes how DSC helps researchers work with them during a project and how the results stay understandable and usable afterward. DSC does not archive everything it supports. Its role is to connect consultation, computing, access, and the repository so that valuable outputs have a path to reuse. How the functions connect
