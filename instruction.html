Instruction Statistics

For DSC staff and library administration: workshop attendance from attendee-level and validated aggregate sources.

Last rendered: September 27, 2026

NoteHeadline figures since 2017

DSC instruction is counted in registered seats: one registration for one session, not a unique person and not an attendance. The series runs from May 2017 through September 2026 (2026 is partial) and includes the UC-wide series DSC co-founded and co-delivers, so UCLA participants are a subset. DSC offered 819 workshop sessions from May 2017 through May 2026. This series replaces the earlier published figure of 17,466 attendee-events (May 2017 to April 2024); the sections after it cover DSC’s attendee-level file only.

NoteHow the seat series is counted
  • Unit: registered seats, one per person per session signed up. One unit is used for each series: UC Carpentries and Library Carpentry count registered seats, Love Data Week counts registered seats (best effort), GIS Week counts registrations, and DSC-run workshops use the larger of a rebuild from named registration sources and the published aggregate, per year
  • Window: May 2017 through September 2026. 2026 is partial because the Fall 2026 UC Carpentries series is in progress
  • Why it differs from the earlier 17,466 (May 2017 to April 2024): earlier Carpentries figures counted attended seats or people, not registered seats; the window now runs through 2026; and Love Data Week 2021 is counted on the same UC-wide basis as 2022-2024 (1,529 registered seats, where the earlier series counted 177 from DSC-hosted sessions only)
  • Open items: Library Carpentry May 2026 attended is 428 here (rejoins merged by name) against 453 in the earlier series; UC Carpentries September 2024 attended is 1,036 Zoom rows at 30 minutes or more, 1,027 after merging rejoins. Named sources for general DSC workshops in 2017-2019 are incomplete, so those years use the published aggregate where it is larger
  • Aggregates only: row-level registration and attendance data stay outside this repository

NoteData notes
  • Sources: Consolidated workshop attendee records (including UC-wide GIS Week and Love Data Week) and privacy-safe aggregate workshop summaries
  • Unit: Attendance instances. LibInsight uses one attendee-event row; aggregate sources provide a validated count for each event. A person attending three workshops contributes three attendance instances
  • UCLA attendees: filtered on institution == "UCLA". Records with unknown institution remain in overall/year/topic totals but are excluded from UCLA, department, and school metrics
  • UCLA-held: UCLA-affiliated records plus records with unknown institution from 2017–2020 sessions classified as held at UCLA (data/reference/instruction/session_venue_2017_2020.tsv, reviewed by the DSC director, 2026-09). Institution was not collected before 2020, so the UCLA-affiliated figure reads zero for 2017–2019 although that teaching was on campus. A UCLA-held session can include a few outside attendees. Sessions classified as mixed (co-hosted or UC-wide) or held elsewhere are excluded. This metric does not feed department or school counts
  • Department: standardized_department field, mapped from raw free-text entries; unmapped values appear as-is
  • Organizational parent: versioned mapping from validated UCLA departments to College divisions, professional schools, the Library, UCLA Health, institutes, or administrative groups. Missing and unresolved departments remain unknown
  • School/division: not inferred when the source lacks a validated department. Unknown is never treated as non-UCLA
  • UC-wide series: GIS Week and Love Data Week are included from the consolidated attendee source; their upstream workbooks are retained as provenance and are not added again
  • Registered seats (headline): a separate reconciled series from data/processed/canonical/instruction_seats_by_program_year.csv, counted as described under “How the seat series is counted”. It is not the sum of the attendee-level tables on this page
  • Small cells: department, organizational-parent, and affiliation breakdowns fold any unit with fewer than 5 into one combined row, which is omitted if it is also under 5
  • Full rules: Metrics Governance · About & methodology
