Where the Center’s users are on campus

People who registered for, attended, or consulted with the Data Science Center come from buildings across campus, from the humanities and social sciences on the north side to the health sciences and engineering on the south.

The counts below are floors, not rates. A person counts once, however many times they took part. How to read these counts explains what is and is not included.

How to read these counts

  • Who is counted. People are placed by the building their department or school sits in. Each person counts once.
  • First column. Everyone with a recorded workshop registration, workshop attendance, consultation, support ticket, or Dataverse deposit. A registration counts even when there is no attendance record, because attendance was not recorded for every session. This column is a floor on people reached, not a count of attendees.
  • Second column. Only people with a recorded attendance, consultation, support ticket, or deposit.
  • Window. Records begin in 2017 for consultations and at different dates for other sources, and run to November 2026.
  • Not counted. People without a usable email address, and people whose department we could not place. UC Love Data Week registrations (2022 to 2024) are not included: those lists have names but no email addresses, so they could not be matched to the other records. DSC staff and DataSquad members, who ran the services, are not counted.
  • Small numbers. A figure is left out when it, or the number of people it leaves out of the building’s total, is under 5. A dash means the figure is not shown. No totals across buildings are shown, so no hidden figure can be worked out by subtraction.
  • Center for Health Sciences: Medicine and Public Health units are spread across the Health Sciences complex; shown at one point.
  • Young Research Library (YRL): Library staff work in several libraries; shown at one point.
  • Math Sciences Building: Point is approximate.
  • Gonda Neuroscience Building: Neuroscience graduate program only; other neuroscience units sit elsewhere.

How the counts were made: metrics governance. Locations come from each department’s published address.
